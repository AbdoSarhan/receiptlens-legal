ReceiptLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

ReceiptLens is a product developed by AMS Apps (Egypt). For any questions regarding privacy: am1535146@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

  • Stored locally on your device: receipt photos and their extracted data (merchant, amount, date, category), and your preferences (language, dark mode, currency, ad settings).
  • Generated during usage: anonymous analytics (app opened, receipt saved, export, ad viewed) via Google Firebase Analytics — no personally identifiable information; crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics; and your Pro subscription status via Google Play Billing (we do not store any payment details).
  • Processed in the cloud: receipt images are sent temporarily to Google Gemini AI for data extraction and are not retained. If the network is unavailable, a local on-device extraction is used instead.

3. How We Use Your Data

Only to make the app work: extracting receipt data, sorting and exporting (CSV/Excel), and enabling the Pro feature. We do not sell or share your data with any third party.

4. Advertising

We use Google AdMob (banner, rewarded, and interstitial ads). Google may show personalized ads according to Google's policies. Ads are removed once you subscribe to Pro.

5. Your Rights and Controls

You can delete any receipt or all of your data permanently from Settings, cancel your subscription from the Google Play Store, or contact us to request deletion of your data.

6. Privacy & Third Parties

Your data is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to our servers. Use of Gemini/Firebase is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Policy Updates

Any changes will be published here with the date updated above.
