Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Who We Are

ReceiptLens is a product developed by AMS Apps (Egypt). Questions: am1535146@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

  • Local storage: receipt photos and extracted data (merchant, amount, date, category), plus preferences (language, dark mode, currency, ad settings).
  • Analytics: anonymous events (app opened, receipt saved, export, ad viewed) via Firebase Analytics — no PII; crash reports via Crashlytics; Pro subscription status via Google Play Billing (no payment data stored).
  • Cloud: receipt images are sent temporarily to Google Gemini AI for extraction and not retained. If offline, local extraction is used instead.

3. How We Use Your Data

Only to make the app work: extracting receipt data, sorting and exporting (CSV/Excel), and enabling Pro. We never sell or share your data.

4. Advertising

We use Google AdMob (banner, rewarded, interstitial). Google may show personalized ads per its policies. Ads are removed when you subscribe to Pro.

5. Your Rights

You can delete any receipt or all your data permanently from Settings, cancel your subscription via Google Play, or contact us to request deletion.

6. Third Parties

Your data stays on your device and is never uploaded to our servers. Gemini/Firebase usage is governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Updates

Changes will be published here with the date updated above.
